Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit the Health Today website. It covers the website and does not describe how the clinic manages patient records.

Website visits and appointments

The website lists doctors, chamber locations, and consultation hours. It has no account registration, online booking form, or facility to submit medical records. If you call a number listed on the site, you communicate directly with the clinic outside this website.

The website’s hosting provider may process technical data, such as IP addresses and page requests, to serve and secure the site.

The website saves your language preference in your browser so it can remember your choice when you return.

Google Maps

The chamber map links open Google Maps outside this website. Health Today does not load an embedded map or send a map request when you browse this site. If you follow a map link, Google handles your visit under its Privacy Policy.

Privacy questions

For questions about website privacy, contact Health Today using the phone numbers in the Appointment section.
